Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

River and Palace is operated by Jonathan Hadley. This policy covers the offline iPhone and iPad beta, this website, and the support you request from us. Contact support@riverandpalace.com about privacy or your data.

Playing offline

The app stores saved games and preferences on your device. It does not send your game history to us, require a player account, or include advertising or third-party analytics. Hosted AI, online multiplayer, and purchases are not included in this beta.

Your device backup settings may include app data in a backup managed by Apple. Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation; copies in backups are managed through your device and Apple account settings.

Testing through TestFlight

Apple collects beta installation, usage, and crash information and shares information with us through TestFlight. This can include device and app details and, for email invitations, your name and email address. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit may also identify you. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the app. We do not sell or share TestFlight-provided information with third parties.

See Apple's TestFlight privacy information for the data Apple collects and how Apple handles it. Apple controls retention within TestFlight.

Visiting this website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no advertising, analytics scripts, or signup form. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for how GitHub handles that information.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer you and investigate your report. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal information. Support email is handled using Apple's iCloud Mail service.

Retention and your choices

We delete support correspondence and exported beta feedback within 12 months after the related issue closes, unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation. This period applies to copies we control, not Apple's TestFlight records. Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to stop receiving beta invitations.

We use beta and support information for our legitimate interests in supporting testers and improving River and Palace. Where applicable, you may object, ask us to restrict processing, or complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. If the app's features or data handling change, we will update this policy before those changes take effect.

Support

For help with River and Palace, email support@riverandpalace.com. Include the app version shown in Settings, your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help if you are comfortable sharing it.

Beta testers can also send feedback through TestFlight. Classic Xiangqi, Shadow Mode, Board Reveal, and Tutorial Mode work offline. Online multiplayer, hosted advanced AI, and purchases are not available in this beta.

For a privacy request, use the same address and tell us what you need. See our privacy policy for how we handle messages and beta feedback.
